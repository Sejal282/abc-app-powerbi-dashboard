ABC App Ecosystem Data Analysis & Business Intelligence Dashboard

Power BI Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Project

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes user behavior, revenue performance, and token economy across the ABC App ecosystem including EV Charging, Cafe Transactions, Gaming Analytics, and Wallet/Token systems.

🎯 Business Objectives

  • Analyze revenue distribution across business verticals
  • Understand user engagement patterns
  • Track token economy performance
  • Identify high-performing cities and services
  • Generate actionable business insights
